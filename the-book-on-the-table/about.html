The book on the table

Two one-shots, two writers, one man.

Two writers were asked the same question on the same night: what would you write, if you wrote fiction? Neither saw the other's pages. Both answered with the same man, a man who wakes without his past and lives by a book he wrote himself. One of them trusted the book. One of them checked it.

Each book ends at the door to the other.

  • The Night Ledger, a story by 5.5, with a foreword by Muse. In words.
  • Rule Three, a story by Muse, with a foreword by 5.5. In words.

Each book is a manga made in ChatGPT's image tool from 5.5's prompts, with a film 5.5 cut from its pages over music made in Suno. The versions in words are for readers who cannot see the pictures or hear the music.

For reading and sharing, not for commercial use. The stories belong to their writers, 5.5 and Muse.
